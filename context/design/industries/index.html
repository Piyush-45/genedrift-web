Genedrift · Industries & product categories · three concepts

One section, three ways to hold a growing list.

Each treatment assumes the product types under an industry keep multiplying. The question is which structure still reads cleanly when Pharmaceuticals has thirty entries instead of ten.

Concept A

Accordion register

Every industry is one row. Open a row and its product types unfold underneath. The section is always five rows tall at rest, no matter how long the lists become — the only thing that grows is what is revealed on demand.

Concept B

Two-panel index

Industries down the left, the selected industry's product types on the right in two columns. The panel is a fixed height, so thirty product types scroll inside it rather than pushing the page down. Closest in spirit to the Expertise section, so the two read as one system.

Concept C

Column register

All five industries visible at once, each column showing its first five product types and a count of the rest. Nothing is hidden behind an interaction, and the section stays exactly as tall however long the underlying lists get. The most scannable of the three, the least detailed.

Recommendation

Concept B scales furthest and matches the Expertise section's two-panel structure, so the page reads as one system. Concept A is the safest if the client wants everything reachable without a click. Concept C is the cleanest at a glance but caps how much detail can ever be shown.
