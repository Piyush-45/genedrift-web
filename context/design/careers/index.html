Life at Genedrift

Small teams, named owners, real authorities.

Nobody here hands work over a wall. The person who writes the dossier answers the queries on it.

You own the market

Regulatory leads carry named markets rather than tickets. You learn one authority properly instead of skimming twelve.

The work is on record

Our QPPVs and local representatives are registered with the authority in their own names. That is a career asset, not just a job.

Nothing is subcontracted

No agency layer between you and the client, and none between you and the regulator. Decisions get made where the knowledge is.

Recruitment process

Five steps, three weeks, no ghosting.

You will hear back after every stage, including the ones that end in a no.

01

Application

Send a CV and tell us which markets and capabilities you have worked in.

02

Screening call

Thirty minutes with the regional lead for the team you applied to.

03

Technical discussion

A real problem from our work — a pathway, a dossier gap, a safety case.

04

Team conversation

Meet the people you would work beside, and ask them anything.

05

Offer
